CipherNest is a zero-knowledge storage app for Android. Files are encrypted on your device before they reach your Google Drive or OneDrive, so no server, including ours, can read them.

- Source: CipherNest, GPL-3.0-or-later
- Website: https://ciphernest.io
- Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.ciphernest.vault
- Security policy and vulnerability reporting: https://ciphernest.io/security
